# 🏥 Healthcare Operations & Patient Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Healthcare Analytics solution developed using Power BI, SQL, DAX, and Power Query. The dashboard provides actionable insights into hospital operations, patient demographics, treatment outcomes, financial performance, physician utilization, and digital acquisition metrics.

The objective is to transform healthcare data into meaningful business intelligence, enabling stakeholders to monitor performance, identify trends, optimize resources, and improve patient care.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor overall hospital performance through centralized KPIs
* Analyze patient demographics and population trends
* Evaluate appointment scheduling and utilization patterns
* Assess physician workload and performance
* Measure departmental efficiency and resource utilization
* Track revenue performance and financial health
* Analyze treatment outcomes and success rates
* Evaluate digital acquisition channels and patient conversion metrics

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

**Patients Overview & Hospital Performance**

Provides a high-level summary of key hospital KPIs including patient volume, appointments, treatments, revenue, and operational performance.

---

### 2. Patient Demographics

**Patient Segmentation & Demographic Analysis**

Analyzes patient distribution by age, gender, city, and demographic trends to better understand the patient population.

---

### 3. Appointments

**Appointment Trends & Scheduling Performance**

Tracks appointment volumes, visit patterns, scheduling efficiency, and patient engagement over time.

---

### 4. Doctors

**Clinical Workforce & Performance Insights**

Evaluates physician profiles, specialization distribution, appointment workload, and doctor performance metrics.

---

### 5. Departments

**Department Operations & Resource Utilization**

Measures departmental performance, patient volumes, treatment distribution, and resource utilization across hospital departments.

---

### 6. Revenue Analytics

**Revenue Performance & Financial Insights**

Monitors revenue trends, department-wise revenue contribution, treatment revenue, and overall financial performance.

---

### 7. Treatment Analytics

**Treatment Outcomes & Success Analysis**

Analyzes treatment volumes, treatment success rates, procedure effectiveness, and patient outcome metrics.

---

### 8. Digital Acquisition

**Patient Acquisition & Digital Engagement**

Tracks website traffic, user engagement, sessions, conversions, bounce rates, and acquisition channel performance.
